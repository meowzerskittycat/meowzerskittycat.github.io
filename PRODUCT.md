# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS (plus a few lines of vanilla JS at most), served directly by GitHub Pages from the repo root. No build step, no framework: a single introduction page does not need one, and it keeps the repo trivially editable.

## Users

People who land on Vivien's personal GitHub Pages site (meowzerskittycat.github.io), typically from the GitHub profile, wanting a quick sense of who Vivien is.

## Product Purpose

A short personal introduction page for Vivien. Not a blog, not a portfolio, no project listings for now. Success: a visitor leaves knowing who Vivien is in a few seconds, and the page feels like Vivien's own.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page, introduction only.
- No links or contact points (confirmed: "No links").
- No filler or placeholder content; nothing about Vivien may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Mood pinned by the user: Nordic / Alpine, minimalistic, clean, moody.
- Pixel art pinned by the user: a pixel-art scene of mountains and Nordic/Alpine nature, placement left to the designer.
- Palette references supplied by the user (binding as vibe): a light ice-blue set (#E3F2FD, #90CAF9, #2196F3, #0D47A1) and a dark fjord set (#0F2B4C, #1C3B5E, #1E5568, #F3F3F3).

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed facts about Vivien, and the only ones the page may state:
- Name: Vivien.
- Mechanical engineer.
- Used to be a musician and a programmer.
- Loves Nordic metal; named bands: Moonsorrow, Korpiklaani, Darkthrone, Enslaved, Taake.
- Politics: removed from the page at Vivien's request; do not add it back.

Absent and must not be fabricated: location, employer, education, instruments played, languages, photos, contact details, projects.

## Product Principles

- Say only what is true; a short page beats a padded one.
- The atmosphere carries the personality; the copy stays plain.
- Nothing to maintain: static, dependency-free, fast.
